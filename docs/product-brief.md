# EnveeSage product brief

Confirmed 10 October 2026: Nigeria-based business; **curated perfumes and samples**, not a marketplace or own-formula brand. EnveeSage is a working name. This phase delivers research and multiple throwaway design prototypes, not a live commerce launch.

## Product premise

Make buying fragrance feel accessible without flattening the depth enthusiasts enjoy. One catalog; optional discovery help; progressively deeper detail. People choose tasks, not an identity label. NGN, optional guest checkout, realistic delivery costs, transparent product evidence and sample-first discovery.

## Audiences & jobs

- Everyday/new buyers: choose a scent using plain descriptions, budget and context; try before committing.
- Enthusiasts: inspect exact concentration/edition, compare, save and eventually journal wear.
- Gift buyers: explore a discovery kit, know arrival estimates and understand return options.
- Budget-conscious buyers: see sample price, bottle price, sizes and total delivered cost.
- Ingredient-conscious buyers: access actual supplied labels, warnings and missing-information status.
- Mobile/low-bandwidth shoppers and assistive-technology users: fast usable browsing with optional imagery, keyboard access and readable layouts.

## Three questions being prototyped

A. Can an editorial storefront earn curiosity and make practical information easy to find?
B. Does a short optional guided finder improve confidence for people who lack scent vocabulary?
C. Can a catalog workspace offer depth without overwhelming first-time buyers?

All versions share the same illustrative data and sample/detail/bag interactions. Differences are structure, priority and discovery model rather than colors alone. There is no winner yet.

## Proposed direction to evaluate

Use A for the storefront, B as an optional finder and C for an enthusiast view. Keep one catalog and checkout under all three. This is a product hypothesis, not a user-validated conclusion.

## MVP scope after a direction is chosen

Responsive website first; consider installable PWA later if repeat use justifies it. Catalog and exact SKUs; search/budget/family filters; samples; sourced product details; private saved list; cart; guest checkout; Nigerian hosted payments; verified order state; supported delivery zones; fair returns/support. Admin catalog, stock, supplier evidence, label versions and fulfillment are part of the production scope.

Keep payments, stock and order state on a server; do not adapt this single HTML file into production checkout. Separate editorial fragrance data from sellable SKUs. Store money in integer NGN minor units. Private collection features must remain distinct from public reviews. No AI algorithm is needed to validate early discovery.

## P0 launch decisions

- Initial cities/states and actual courier serviceability.
- Authorized suppliers, assortment, actual price points and asset/data permissions.
- Manufacturer-supplied sample kits vs decants: validate NAFDAC, packaging and permissions for the chosen format.
- Registration and advertising evidence; verify current regulations and their applicability to the actual operating model.
- Tax treatment and total-price presentation with Nigerian accounting/legal advice.
- Merchant onboarding, verified payment lifecycle and refund procedures.
- Returns, damaged/wrong items, complaint channels and customer support ownership.
- NDPC privacy assessment, hosting/processor locations, optional marketing and analytics consent.
- WCAG 2.2 AA as engineering quality target, with actual audits and user checks.

## Validation with Nigerian participants

Recruit across intents rather than assigning “basic” vs “expert” identities. Include mobile-first users and at least some assistive-technology use. Alternate variant order to reduce ordering bias. Use the same tasks:

1. Find a fresh everyday fragrance within ₦50,000; locate sample and bottle prices.
2. Pick two samples; understand delivered total before any payment step.
3. Compare two scents and explain the differences.
4. Locate ingredients and distinguish them from notes; explain what missing data means.
5. Choose a gift discovery set and find the delivery estimate/returns information.

Record task completion, hesitation, misinterpretation, cost understanding and purchase confidence. A visual preference vote alone is insufficient. Real inventory/price research and Nigerian customer interviews remain to be done.

## Research boundary

Regulatory, safety, privacy, accessibility and payment facts are cited in the standards report. Desk research does not validate local demand, price willingness, scent preference, recommendation accuracy, supplier legitimacy or legal launch clearance.
