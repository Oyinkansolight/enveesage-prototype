# Fragrance standards and product decisions — Nigeria first

Research date: **10 October 2026**. All linked sources below were checked on this date unless an access limitation is recorded. Scope: a Nigerian fragrance discovery, sampling, commerce and collection product. This is product research; the operational launch requires confirming the current rules against the actual seller/importer/manufacturer model.

## Decision

Build one inclusive catalogue with progressive detail: a plain-language discovery path, a technical enthusiast workspace, and a gift path. Start with Nigeria, NGN and explicitly supported delivery zones. Treat verified product information, delivery cost, sample availability and preference uncertainty as core product features. A discovery prototype can validate these flows before real inventory, payments or compliance claims exist.

**Product recommendations** throughout this report are design proposals, not regulations. **Requirements** are attributed to a named authority and geography. International export material is secondary to Nigeria.

## Nigeria launch requirements

### NAFDAC: registration, imports and advertising

**Requirement / official guidance.** NAFDAC's imported-cosmetics guideline says cosmetics cannot be manufactured, imported, exported, advertised, sold or distributed in Nigeria without registration. Imported-product applications include manufacturer authorization and documentation, and approval follows documentation review, GMP assessment and laboratory analysis. Registration lasts five years under this guideline. Registration does not itself grant an advertising permit: a separate application and approval are required. The PDF has an effective date of 1 June 2018 and review date of 31 May 2023; verify subsequent replacement before operational launch. [NAFDAC imported cosmetics registration guideline, sections 1–10](https://www.nafdac.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/Files/Resources/Guidelines/R_and_R_Guidelines/IIMPORTS/Guidelines-for-the-Registration-of-Imported-Cosmetics.pdf), checked 2026-10-10.

**Requirement / official guidance.** NAFDAC also publishes a local-cosmetics registration pathway, including product labels, certificate of analysis and facility inspection. It repeats the distinction between registration and advertising approval. Manufacturing or repackaging locally therefore needs its own assessment rather than assuming a resale model applies. [NAFDAC cosmetics made in Nigeria guideline](https://www.nafdac.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/Files/Resources/Guidelines/R_and_R_Guidelines/LOCAL/Guidelines-for-the-Registration-of-Cosmetics-made-in-Nigeria.pdf), checked 2026-10-10.

**Recommendation.** Launch with authorized suppliers and sealed, documented products. Prefer manufacturer-supplied sample kits initially. Do not assume decanting a registered bottle makes a separately packaged sample compliant. Resolve decant registration, packaging, trademark permission and manufacturing/storage obligations with NAFDAC before selling that format. Product pages can show an actual registration number and its provenance; never generate plausible numbers. Registration evidence alone should not become an authenticity guarantee for every physical bottle.

**Primary source / verification boundary.** NAFDAC's NAPAMS portal offers verification by registration number. This establishes an official verification route, not a documented public API integration or proof of cosmetics catalogue coverage. [NAPAMS](https://registration.nafdac.gov.ng/Home/), checked 2026-10-10.

### Nigerian cosmetics labels

**Requirement.** NAFDAC's published Cosmetics Products Labelling Regulations 2019 cover inner/outer labels as applicable. They specify identity, brand where applicable, batch/lot, net content, expiry, manufacturer/origin, registration number and storage conditions. Ingredient declarations must be readable; fragrance has special declaration provisions, and known hypersensitivity ingredients must be declared. Net contents use the metric system. Directions, warnings and claims have further provisions. An app's information panel does not replace physical packaging requirements. [NAFDAC Cosmetics Products Labelling Regulations 2019, regulations 2–14](https://www.nafdac.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/Files/Resources/Regulations/COSMETIC_REGULATIONS/Cosmetics-Products-Labelling-Regulations-2019.pdf), checked 2026-10-10.

**Recommendation.** Store package-label text/photo against the exact concentration, size, market and version. Keep marketed scent notes separate from ingredients. Provide “Ingredients not supplied” and “Information last checked…” states. Do not infer absence of an allergen from absence of a displayed ingredient, a missing dataset or a pleasant note description.

### FCCPC: accurate sales, understandable policies and redress

**Requirement.** The Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act protects cancellation of advance orders subject to reasonable charges (s120), correspondence with description/sample (s121), qualifying returns (s122), and freedom from misleading marketing (s123). It addresses unfair contractual terms (s127) and conspicuous risk notices (s128). Returns depend on grounds and circumstances; this is not evidence of a universal unconditional change-of-mind period. [FCCPA 2018, Part XV](https://fccpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/FCCPA-2018.pdf), checked 2026-10-10.

**Recommendation.** Show supplier identity, accurate size/concentration, product price, delivery and total before payment. Separate “I changed my mind” from damaged, wrong, defective or misdescribed goods. Avoid blanket “no refunds” copy. Explain opened-product rules and complaint evidence without purporting to remove statutory rights. Preserve invoices, batches, dispatch evidence and support decisions. For gifts, show the delivery window and a recipient-safe receipt option.

### Nigeria privacy: NDP Act and final GAID 2025

**Requirement.** NDPC states that the NDP Act applies to organizations operating in Nigeria, processing in Nigeria, or processing Nigerian data subjects even from outside Nigeria. [NDPC official FAQs](https://ndpc.gov.ng/faqs/), checked 2026-10-10.

**Requirement.** The final GAID 2025 lists lawful bases and requires consent in specified cases including direct marketing, sensitive personal data and children's data (articles 16–18). Nonnecessary cookies require affirmative accept/reject options; the necessary-cookie exception is constrained (article 19). Its DPIA provisions expressly include profiling/evaluation and sensitive data, and cross-border transfers require assessment (articles 28, 45). [NDPC final GAID 2025](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/NDP-ACT-GAID-2025-MARCH-20TH.pdf), checked 2026-10-10. Browser extraction failed; the official PDF was successfully downloaded and its text inspected directly. The older **draft** GAID was not used as the controlling source.

**Recommendation.** Offer guest discovery. Keep scent likes/dislikes separate from health history. Avoid collecting diagnosis, asthma or detailed allergy history in the initial quiz. If a health-related exclusion feature becomes necessary, obtain explicit optional consent, conduct the DPIA review, limit access/retention and offer deletion. Explain personalization inputs and let users reset them. Document lawful purposes for orders and support; use separate marketing consent. Map hosting, analytics, recommendations, couriers and payment processors, including cross-border data flows. Evaluate NDPC registration/DPO/audit obligations for the actual scale and business category before launch.

## Fragrance information and safety boundaries

### IFRA is not consumer-product certification

**Industry framework.** IFRA says a Certificate of Conformity is established by the company making a fragrance mixture for its customer and intended use; IFRA does not issue it. It does not replace a safety assessment or local legal compliance. Standards are voluntary at the legal-framework level. [IFRA certification explanation](https://ifrafragrance.org/initiatives-positions/safe-use-fragrance-science/ifra-standards/certification-of-ifra-standards), checked 2026-10-10.

**Industry framework.** IFRA limits are expressed in the finished consumer product, and EDT/EDP/cologne names have no universally fixed international concentration definitions. Its illustrative concentration ranges overlap; they describe industry practice. [IFRA terminology and limits FAQ](https://ifrafragrance.org/transparency-list/about-the-ifra-transparency-list), checked 2026-10-10.

**Recommendation.** Never place an “IFRA certified” badge on a prototype bottle. If evidence exists, name the supplier declaration, amendment, product category, date and scope accurately. Model concentration as a manufacturer-declared label plus optional documented percentage. Do not calculate guaranteed longevity from EDP/EDT. Define projection as perceived reach and sillage as perceived trail in original educational copy, with editorial ownership and review.

### Sensitive users need evidence, not a safety score

**Health evidence.** FDA's consumer guidance states cosmetics can cause allergic reactions; ingredient labels may leave individual perfume ingredients undisclosed. It cautions that US “hypoallergenic”, “fragrance-free” and “for sensitive skin” claims lack federal definitions. This is US guidance, not Nigerian claim law. [FDA allergens in cosmetics](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetic-ingredients/allergens-cosmetics), checked 2026-10-10.

**Recommendation.** Avoid “safe for sensitive skin”, “allergy-safe”, “clean = safer”, or a personalized medical compatibility percentage. Provide factual ingredient disclosure and its limitations. Distinguish dislikes from diagnosed sensitivity. A sample is a purchasing aid, not a guarantee against reaction; do not advise home patch testing as a diagnostic substitute. Let users browse without being pushed to buy. Health copy needs appropriate local review before production.

### Notes, taxonomy and rights

**First-party licensing boundary.** Fragrances of the World describes a commercial catalogue organized with Michael Edwards' Fragrance Wheel and expressly prohibits copying, reproduction, database entry and derivative works without consent. Public availability is not an app-data license. [Fragrances of the World database and terms notice](https://fragrancesoftheworld.info/), checked 2026-10-10.

**Recommendation.** Use original taxonomy/visuals, licensed catalogue feeds, or authorized manufacturer data. Record source URL, retrieved date, rights basis and attribution for every image, description, note list and classification. Do not scrape an enthusiast site as a default catalogue. “Citrus”, “woody”, “floral” can be our navigation concepts without reproducing a copyrighted wheel layout or bulk classifications. Store `brand_declared`, `editorial` and `community_reported` facts separately. Never equate a marketed “rose note” with a chemical formula, allergen declaration or natural rose content.

## Inclusive product design and accessibility

**Technical standard, proposed quality target.** WCAG 2.2 AA includes keyboard access, visible focus, text contrast, reflow, focus not being obscured, target-size rules (24 × 24 CSS pixels with exceptions), and accessible authentication. Normal text contrast is generally 4.5:1; large text 3:1. A floating prototype variant selector must not obscure focused content. WCAG is a conformance standard; this report does not establish a Nigeria-specific legal mandate to that exact version. [W3C WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/), checked 2026-10-10.

**Recommendations / hypotheses to test.**

| User intent | Entry language | Detail available | Success task |
|---|---|---|---|
| Newcomer/everyday shopper | “Fresh and easy”, occasion, budget | Explain notes and concentration inline | Find two suitable samples without knowing perfume vocabulary |
| Enthusiast | Notes, house, concentration, comparison | Sources, editions, accords, optional wear journal | Compare exact variants and organize owned/wanted/tried |
| Gift buyer | Recipient taste, budget, arrival date | Gift-ready kits, returns, recipient preferences | Buy a low-risk discovery gift with a clear arrival window |
| Budget-focused shopper | NGN budget, size, sample option | Price per ml with like-for-like context | Understand total cost without premium-price pressure |
| Ingredient-conscious user | Ingredient visibility | Exact label, date, missing-data state | Locate evidence without a false safety assurance |
| Low-bandwidth/mobile user | Fast text-led content | Optional images and short explanations | Complete discovery and checkout without heavy media |

Do not label people “basic” in the product. Let them choose their desired detail at any point. Do not require a gender choice. Treat occasion and weather preferences as user inputs, not deterministic personality/climate science. Support keyboard and screen-reader use, reduced motion, text zoom, labeled controls and accessible status announcements. Design for 44–48px touch targets as our usability preference beyond the AA minimum. Test with real Nigerian participants; none of these user preferences is established by desk research alone.

## Nigeria payments and delivery

**Provider capability.** Paystack's official documentation supports a Nigeria pay-by-transfer channel and temporary checkout accounts, with provider-specific expiry and handling of wrong amounts. This is not evidence that every merchant account is already approved/enabled. [Paystack Pay with Transfer, edited 16 September 2026](https://support.paystack.com/en/articles/2128642), checked 2026-10-10.

**Integration requirement.** Paystack advises server-side transaction verification, with pending/ongoing/failed/reversed states; a browser callback is not sufficient confirmation. [Paystack payment verification](https://paystack.com/docs/payments/verify-payments/), checked 2026-10-10. Webhook origin can be validated using the `x-paystack-signature` HMAC SHA512 signature; retries mean processing must tolerate repeated delivery. [Paystack webhooks](https://paystack.com/docs/payments/webhooks/), checked 2026-10-10.

**Recommendation.** Use provider-hosted checkout and NGN integer minor units. Verify reference, amount and currency on the server, then fulfill once. Model checkout, payment and order separately. Include a calm “Awaiting confirmation” state and recovery after navigation/network loss. Do not collect card data or BVN for an ordinary perfume checkout. A prototype's checkout button should explicitly simulate these states.

**Logistics fact.** DHL explains that many alcohol-containing perfumes are subject to dangerous-goods shipping requirements; classification depends on composition/flammability, and country rules vary. This does not establish a domestic Nigerian courier's acceptance or rate. [DHL perfume shipping guide](https://www.dhl.com/discover/en-global/logistics-advice/import-export-advice/ship-perfume-internationally), checked 2026-10-10.

**Recommendation.** Obtain written perfume acceptance, packaging and damage policies from actual couriers. Start with confirmed locations rather than inventing “nationwide next day”. Ask for state/city/address and optional landmark, validate a quote before payment, expose delivery estimates as estimates, and retain tracking/support channels. Sample size does not automatically waive transport restrictions. International shipping needs a separate country/product/carrier eligibility gate.

## International expansion: secondary requirements

**EU requirement.** Regulation (EU) 2023/1545 expands fragrance-allergen labeling. The Commission states thresholds of 0.001% for leave-on and 0.01% for rinse-off products, adds 56 allergens, and differentiates placing on the market from continuing availability: noncompliant products cannot newly be placed after the July 2026 transition; qualifying earlier stock has a transition to 31 July 2028. At this research date, the first deadline has already passed. This is not an automatic Nigerian label requirement. [European Commission explanation](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/cosmetics/cosmetic-products-specific-topics/fragrance-allergens-labelling_en), [binding Regulation 2023/1545](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=CELEX%3A32023R1545), checked 2026-10-10.

**US requirement.** FDA explains MoCRA obligations including safety substantiation, facility registration/product listing and adverse-event reporting, with rulemaking for fragrance-allergen labeling. Cosmetics generally are not FDA preapproved (some color additives are an exception). Do not use an “FDA approved perfume” badge. Applicability and exemptions depend on the operator; this research does not establish the final US fragrance-allergen rule's implementation status. [FDA cosmetics and US law](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetics-laws-regulations/cosmetics-us-law), checked 2026-10-10.

**EU privacy reference.** GDPR Article 3 describes territorial scope, including targeting/monitoring certain EU users; Articles 5/6 concern principles/lawful bases and Article 9 special-category data. An EU audience is an additional legal-scope question, not a reason to replace the Nigerian framework with a generic GDPR banner. [GDPR official text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng), checked 2026-10-10; browser rendering of this page was limited. Confirm exact scope and current national implementation before targeting EU customers.

## Proposed production data model

This schema is a design inference from the evidence above, not an externally mandated database design.

| Entity | Core fields | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Fragrance | house, name, original editorial summary, declared notes, taxonomy version | Discovery identity |
| FragranceVariant | concentration label, optional sourced percentage, edition/market, formulation/label version | Avoid conflating EDT, EDP, reformulations |
| Offer/SKU | variant, sealed/sample/decant format, ml, NGN amount, supplier, stock, sales eligibility | Shopping data distinct from editorial catalogue |
| LabelEvidence | exact ingredient text/photo, source, market, batch applicability, reviewed date, rights | Evidence and missing-data handling |
| RegulatoryEvidence | jurisdiction, registration number, issuer/supplier, evidence file, scope, expiry, reviewed date; advertising approval separate | Accurate status instead of generic trust badge |
| SourceRights | URL, owner, license/permission, attribution, permitted usage, retrieval date | Images/data licensing |
| PreferenceProfile | optional likes/dislikes, budget, occasion, provenance, consent/version, reset/deletion | User-controlled personalization |
| Recommendation | input snapshot, reason codes, rule/model version, evidence level, sponsorship flag | Explainability; no invented match probability |
| CollectionEntry | owned/wanted/tried, exact variant, private notes, wear-context | Enthusiast utility without forcing purchase |
| PaymentAttempt | provider reference, amount/currency, status, verified event IDs | Pending/failure and duplicate-event handling |
| Order/Shipment | immutable line/totals snapshot, batch allocated, serviceability quote, courier, delivery estimate, status | Reconciliation and support |
| Return/Complaint | ground, requested remedy, evidence, decision, refund state | Fair redress and audit trail |

Do not add a numeric `safety_score` or `guaranteed_longevity_hours`. Store user wear reports as contextual subjective observations with sample counts; do not fabricate review totals, purchase counts or “98% match”. An explainable “Suggested because you selected citrus + everyday + this budget” is sufficient for initial rule-based discovery.

## Delivery priorities

**P0 before real commerce:** confirm legal business role; supplier/registration/advertising evidence; actual label data and asset rights; clear returns/support; NGN full totals; genuine courier eligibility; verified payment state machine; NDPC privacy assessment and consent; WCAG-oriented audit; real stock.

**P1 initial user value:** short optional quiz, budget/occasion filters, product detail with sources, samples and gift discovery, exact-variant compare, private saved collection, missing-information states.

**P2 after validation:** community reviews, advanced wear logs, similarity recommendations and replenishment. Community imports, public profiles and AI explanations require additional moderation, licensing and privacy decisions.

## Prototype experiments

1. **Editorial boutique:** test whether visual storytelling makes newcomers comfortable while budget, sample and delivery information remain findable.
2. **Guided discovery:** test whether everyday language and an optional short quiz improve shortlist confidence without falsely implying scent prediction.
3. **Enthusiast atlas:** test whether comparison and collection affordances support depth without making the initial experience intimidating.

Keep the same catalogue/tasks across variants so comparison is meaningful. Evaluate mobile and keyboard flows as well as aesthetic preference. Ask participants to find an everyday option under a stated NGN budget, build a sample shortlist, compare two exact variants, find ingredients, and choose a gift with a deadline. Record task completion, hesitation, understanding of total cost and confidence; avoid treating a visual vote as proof of usability.

## Unknowns and limits

- Nigeria and a curated perfume/sample retail model are confirmed. EnveeSage is the working name. Launch city, inventory ownership, importer/manufacturer involvement, sample sourcing and supplier relationships remain open.
- No Nigerian user interviews or demand/pricing study were performed. No assumption about universal local scent, gender or weather preferences is validated.
- NAFDAC documents are publicly available but some review dates have passed. Resolve whether newer gazetted rules supersede them and how online editorial/product advertising is treated. No exemption for decants was established.
- No NAFDAC API contract, integration permission or definitive full perfume verification coverage was established.
- No courier quotes, stock prices, tax analysis, Paystack merchant onboarding or actual registration evidence was obtained. Prototype amounts and deliveries must be marked illustrative.
- EU legal pages sometimes had limited rendering; the Commission's contemporary explanation supported the date/threshold summary. US allergen final-rule status remains unresolved. GDPR is an expansion pointer, not a completed territorial-scope opinion.
- Desk research establishes standards and constraints; it does not establish an optimal recommendation algorithm, individual medical safety, or performance promises.
